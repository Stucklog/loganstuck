Evidence for better programmes

Household surveys. Programme evaluation. Shared decisions.

Logan is a public health evaluation and evidence specialist with a PhD in epidemiology and a master's degree in biostatistics. His work brings together household surveys, monitoring and evaluation, applied statistics, and GIS to assess how programmes reach people and where they can improve.

His focus is programme evaluation with institutions and researchers in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). Malaria is his strongest subject area, alongside experience in tuberculosis and broader global health. He has more than a year of cumulative professional experience in sub-Saharan Africa, including living in Zanzibar.

Core work

From study design to programme decisions

Household and population surveys

Study design, sampling, questionnaires, ODK data collection, field-team training, data-quality systems, and analysis that accounts for complex survey designs.

Programme evaluation and learning

Coverage, impact, and process evaluations; implementation research; and monitoring, evaluation, research, and learning to help teams understand and improve programmes.

GIS and evidence for decisions

Spatial analysis, geographic targeting, and reproducible analysis in R. Clear maps, reports, and evidence briefs for governments, NGOs, implementers, and funders.
