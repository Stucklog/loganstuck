How engagements can work

Focused support from question to decision.

  1. Clarify the decision. Agree the questions with partner institutions and the people who will use the findings.
  2. Design the evidence plan. Match the survey or evaluation design, indicators, data sources, and fieldwork plan to the question.
  3. Collect, analyse, and interpret. Support data quality and rigorous analysis, then interpret the results with partners.
  4. Communicate for use. Turn findings into useful evidence products, practical recommendations, and stronger team capacity.
